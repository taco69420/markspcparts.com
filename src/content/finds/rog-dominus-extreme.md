Picked this up as a "broken" board — seller described it as power cycling once before booting into Windows, Aura RGB acting erratic, and missing chipset drivers. Diagnosed and fixed it myself: updated the ME Engine firmware, installed chipset drivers, and flashed the BIOS.

Ran it as a daily driver for two months without a single issue. Eventually pulled the GPU and SSD to move on to a new build. When I went to fire it back up with a Titan RTX it hung at POST code 00 — turned out to be corrupted CMOS data, cleared with a single button press and it came straight up.

Comes complete with all accessories, most still unopened. Even the coaster is still sealed. One of the most extreme enthusiast boards ASUS ever made, built around the only consumer 28-core CPU Intel ever shipped.

*Still in the collection — full teardown and VRM deep-dive on the blog.*
