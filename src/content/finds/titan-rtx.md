Picked this up from a local collector off Facebook Marketplace for $500. The guy was a true collector — bought it new and only ever powered it on to test it.

This card is in museum condition. The shroud is flawless, the gold accents are pristine, no dust, no wear. Genuinely one of the cleanest Titan RTX units you'll find anywhere.

The Titan RTX was NVIDIA's top-of-line consumer Turing card — 24 GB of GDDR6, NVLink support for dual-GPU configs, and the full TU102 die. It was never really a gaming card; it was a workstation chip with a consumer shroud. That's what makes it interesting to collect.

*Currently considering it for a dual-NVLink build. Not going anywhere for now.*
